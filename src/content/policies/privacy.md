This website does not ask you to create an account and does not store your personal details.

- **Your bag and wishlist** are saved only in your own browser on your device.
- **When you order or enquire,** the website opens WhatsApp, Instagram or your phone dialler with the message filled in. What you send there is handled by those apps and by us to process your order.
- **Visit statistics:** if switched on, we count page visits and button taps without cookies and without identifying you.
- **Contact:** for any privacy question, write to us using the details on the contact page.
